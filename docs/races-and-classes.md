# Dungeon Crawler Carl RPG — Races, Classes & Custom Point Build System

## Overview

When crawlers survive through the tutorial floors and reach the **Third Floor**, the Dungeon System AI presents them with a milestone choice: **Race and Class Selection**. 

The AI typically recommends three specific Race and Class options based on the crawler's tutorial performance, background skills, and audience entertainment value. Players may choose among the official options presented in the core rulebook (pages 128–157) or utilize the official **Point Build System** (pages 158–164) to construct a custom Race or Class.

## 1. Races System & Mechanics (Pages 128–143)

### Heritage: Earth-Based vs. Alien Races

The World Dungeon categorizes playable species into two primary heritages:

1. **Earth-Based Races (23 Canonical Races)**:
   - Includes natural Earth species (Human, Cat), humanoid fantasy/mythological adaptations (Amazonian, Dwarf, Elf, Sasquatch, Frost Maiden), shapeshifters (Changeling, Doppelgänger), demonic/primal forms (Changbi Demon, Lajabless, Primal, Obsidian Butterfly), and dungeon mutations (Arachnid, Crocodilian, Igneous, Rat Hooligan, Tetrakai, Tigran).
   - **Silver Earth Box**: Earth species receive a guaranteed Silver Earth Box upon selection, containing an **Earth Hobby Potion** (grants 3 Ranks in a designated Earth hobby skill).
   - **Earth Class Access**: Grants unrestricted access to all Earth-based classes.

2. **Alien Syndicate Races (7 Canonical Races)**:
   - Species hailing from across the Syndicate galaxy: **Bune**, **Caprid**, **Grulke**, **Hobgoblin**, **Pocket Kuma**, **Pterolykos**, and **Skyfowl**.
   - **Galactic Popularity**: Alien crawlers possess planetary fanbases who cheer for their home species, providing unique opportunities to build **Popularity** and AI favor.
   - **Class Restrictions**: Alien species cannot select Earth-exclusive classes; they must choose alien or universally accessible dungeon archetypes.

### Standardized Creature Size Categories

Every race possesses an official size rating that determines physical presence, carry capacity, and spatial mechanics:
- **Size 1 (Tiny)**
- **Size 2 (Small)**: e.g., Cat, Pocket Kuma
- **Size 3 (Petite)**: e.g., Frost Maiden, Rat Hooligan
- **Size 4 (Medium)**: Standard humanoid baseline (Human, Elves, Dwarves, Amazonian, Bune, Hobgoblin, etc.)
- **Size 5 (Large)**: e.g., Igneous, Sasquatch, Caprid
- **Size 6 (Huge)**

## 4. Official Race & Class Point Build System (Pages 158–164)

### Core Rules & Starting Budgets

When players want to build a custom Race or Class:
- **Race Build Budget**: **25 Race Build Points**
- **Class Build Budget**: **30 Class Build Points**
- **Strict Separation**: Race points and Class points exist in completely separate pools; you **cannot** transfer points between them.
- **Leftover Points**: Unspent points are lost upon finalization.

### Benefits Menu & Point Costs

Benefits are categorized into five tiers based on impact:

#### 1. Minor Benefits (1 Build Point each)
- `+1 to any Ability Score` (STR, DEX, CON, INT, CHA).
- Membership in *Club Desperado* or *Club Vanquisher*.
- +1 bonus to Skill Advancement Checks for a specific Skill at floor end.
- Raise a specific Skill rank cap to Rank 20.
- Add Stat Modifier a second time when using a non-combat skill under specific circumstances.
- Conditional combat advantage (e.g., Advantage when attacking stone creatures).
- Darkvision (see in total darkness) or natural light production.
- Double Mana regeneration when resting in a specific environment.
- Extended visual range (see twice as far).
- Tier-1 Crafting Table granted in Personal Space.
- Free room in Safe Rooms.
- Store discount (+1 sales bonus, purchase discount, or interest).
- Once-per-session non-combat utility trigger.

#### 2. Moderate Benefits (2 Build Points each)
- `+1 in a Skill or Spell` (Active or Passive; max 5 points total of Passive Skills in any Class build).
- Heal 1 Health Bar slot upon damaging/killing a foe with a specific weapon/spell (up to 5 bars/combat).
- Add Stat Modifier a second time on an attack roll in common circumstances.
- Roll with Advantage on Skill Advancement Checks for a specific Skill.
- +1 to Skill Advancement Checks for a thematically linked group of skills.
- Declare damage type alteration for an entire combat encounter at encounter start.
- `+1 DR Buff` (up to a maximum of +3 DR total).
- Universal Guild Access for a specific type (e.g. all Weapon Training Guilds).
- Unrestricted double Mana regeneration from mending and resting.
- Membership in all professional and social clubs.
- Membership in the *Dungeon Book of the Floor Club*.
- Raise a thematically linked group of skills to Rank 20.
- Gold bounty: Mobs drop $1 \times \text{Floor Number}$ extra gold when killed with a specific attack.
- Popularity generation tied to signature actions or attacks.
- Patron Benefit.
- Resistance to an uncommon damage type (Force, Psychic, Necrotic; half damage).
- Resistance to environmental hazards (Falling, Drowning, traps).
- Stat Swap: Replace the ability score used for an ability or skill (e.g., use CHA for Intimidate).
- Add secondary Stat Modifier to a skill in addition to normal modifier.
- Cross-weapon skill transfer (use skill designed for one weapon type on another).
- Enhanced healing: Targets heal 1 additional Health Bar from your healing spells.
- Unchecked terrain traversal (climb, swim, or rough ground without skill checks).
- Natural burrowing or underwater breathing.
- Natural attack at $[\text{Rank} = \text{Floor}]$ dealing $1\text{d}6 + \text{Mod}$ damage ($+1\text{d}6$ at Rank 5, 10, 15).
- Reassign Race or Class stat bonuses once per floor during a Long Rest.

#### 3. Major Benefits (3 Build Points each)
- High-level Manager assistance patron benefit.
- Friendly Pet or Mount granted immediately.
- Pet/Mount Commander Buff: Grant up to 4 points of buffs to all owned pets or mounts.
- Dramatic Encounter Alteration (once per day; e.g. turn enemies against each other).
- Daily Absolute Defense (once per day: negate all damage from a single attack).
- Daily Party Buff (e.g. party-wide regeneration or +1 DR for 1 hour).
- Limited Flight capability.
- Anaerobic biology: Survive without breathing, immune to inhaled toxins.
- **Rage Benefit**: Melee attacks deal +1 damage for each Health Bar slot lost.
- Size 2 (Small) or smaller category.
- +5 ft Move or +5 Step bonus.
- Add $+1\text{d}4$ bonus die to Evade or Stat Checks for a specific stat.
- Natural attack at $[\text{Rank} = \text{Floor}]$ dealing $1\text{d}8 + \text{Mod}$ damage ($+1\text{d}8$ at Rank 5, 10, 15).
- Advantage on a specific non-combat skill or spell.
- Situational double damage multiplier for one round.
- Swap Constitution with another stat for calculating Health Bar capacity.
- Climb movement across walls and ceilings.

#### 4. Extreme Benefits (4 Build Points each)
- +1 in an exotic skill normally unlearnable (e.g., *Cockroach*, *Light on Your Feet*).
- `+1 to all Skills in a thematically linked group of 3–5 Skills` (e.g., all Hand-to-Hand skills).
- Advantage on all Stat Checks for a specific stat.
- Raise all skills in a thematically linked group of up to 6 skills to Rank 20.
- Absolute Immunity to Poison damage and conditions.
- Resistance to a common damage type (Bludgeoning, Piercing, Slashing, Fire).
- Double duration of Rank 5 and lower duration-based spells.
- Broad non-combat advantage under specific circumstances.
- Additional functional arm.
- Doppelgänger shape-changing malleable form.

#### 5. Epic Benefits (6 Build Points each)
- `+1 to all Skills in a thematically linked group of 6+ Skills`.
- Unrestricted Rank 20 cap for all skills on the character.
- Major Defensive Immunity: Full immunity to a common damage type (Bludgeoning, Fire) or half damage from the first 9 attacks each day.
- Limb Regeneration trait.
- Immunity to Poison and all biological diseases.
- Changeling shapeshifting library and identity assimilation.
- Roll with Advantage on all Skill Checks for a specific ability score.
- Unrestricted, unlimited Flight.

### Step Three: Detriments & Extra Build Points

Crawlers can accept mechanical drawbacks to gain additional Build Points:
- **Maximum Detriments**: Crawlers cannot gain more than **+5 extra Build Points** from Race detriments and **+5 extra Build Points** from Class detriments (maximum $+10$ total across character creation).
- **Stat Detriments**: $+1$ extra Build Point for every $-2$ penalty assigned to ability scores.
- **Skill Detriments**: $+1$ extra Build Point for every $-2$ penalty to existing skills, or $+1$ extra Build Point for every $-1$ penalty applied to all skills under a single ability score.

#### Detriment Tiers:
- **Minor Detriments (1 Extra Build Point)**:
  - Disqualified from class due to specific club membership.
  - Every $-2$ stat penalty.
  - Every $-2$ skill penalty.
  - Every $-1$ to all skills under a specific stat.
  - $+1$ Mana cost to spells not favored by class.
  - Conditional disadvantage on a linked skill group.
  - Mandatory deity worship (or prohibited from deity worship).
  - $-1$ penalty to Skill Advancement Checks for a Rank 5+ class skill.
  - Vulnerability to uncommon damage type (take double damage from Force or Necrotic).
  - Halve damage dealt by a non-focus damage category (e.g. Barbarian halving spell damage).
  - Milestone delay (ability only activates on Floor 6 or Level 50+).
- **Moderate Detriments (2 Extra Build Points)**:
  - Broad disadvantage condition (e.g., Disadvantage on all social checks).
  - Skill advancement lockout (skill only ranks up upon descending to the next floor).
  - Severe weapon restrictions.
  - No Stat Modifier added to class weapon damage.
  - $+3$ Mana cost to class spell category.
  - Disadvantage on all movement-related skills.
  - Prohibited from using broad gear categories (e.g. no armor or no passive spells).
- **Major Detriments (3 Extra Build Points)**:
  - Hard stat cap: Ability score is permanently capped at 10 (gear/potions cannot exceed 10).
  - Common damage vulnerability: Take double damage from Bludgeoning, Piercing, or Slashing.

### Walkthrough Example: "Dungeon Dad" (Pages 161–162)

Player Bob constructed the canonical custom class **Dungeon Dad** (Class Types: `Bard`, `Fighter`):
1. **Grill Dad Buff (4 BP)**: Extreme benefit granting a cooked meal party buff (+1 to all skill checks for 1 hour).
2. **Protective Dad (10 BP)**: Rank 3 in Catcher skill (6 BP) + Rank 2 in chosen weapon skill (4 BP).
3. **Helpful Advice Dad (6 BP)**: Rank 2 in Tactics skill (4 BP) + Help/Intervene action bonus die of $+1\text{d}4$ (2 BP).
4. **Fix-It Dad (4 BP)**: Rank 2 in Repair skill (4 BP).
5. **DILF Aura (4 BP)**: Rank 2 in Hot Stuff Aura spell (4 BP; capped at passive skill budget).
6. **Damage Reduction (2 BP)**: +1 DR (2 BP).
7. **Stat Allocation (4 BP)**: +2 Charisma, +1 Strength, +1 Constitution (4 BP).
   - *Total Base Spent*: 34 BP (Exceeds base 30 budget by 4).
8. **Detriments Taken (+2 Extra BP)**:
   - Clumsy dad: $-2$ Dexterity (nets $+1$ BP).
   - Aching bones: Vulnerability to Necrotic damage (nets $+1$ BP).
   - *Adjusted Budget*: $30 + 2 = 32$ BP.
   - Bob adjusted DR to +2 DR (spending the extra 2 BP from detriments).
9. **Final Package**:
   - `STR +1`, `CON +1`, `CHA +2`, `DEX −2`.
   - `Catcher R3`, `Repair R2`, `Tactics R2`, `Weapon R2`, `Hot Stuff Aura R2`.
   - `+2 DR`.
   - Cooked meal party buff (+1 buff to all checks for 1 hour).
   - $+1\text{d}4$ on Help/Intervene actions.
   - Vulnerability: Necrotic.
   - Silver Earth Box with guaranteed Earth Hobby Skill Potion.

## 6. Custom Class Creator Studio (`DCCClassCreatorApp`)

The **Class Creator Studio** (Option A: *"Terminal Ledger" Accordion Studio*) provides an interactive creation terminal for building legal or homebrew classes:

### Architecture & Design
- **Base Point Engine (`DCCBasePointBuilderApp`)**:
  - Reusable point accounting foundation shared between the Class Creator (30 BP base budget) and the Race Creator (25 BP base budget).
  - Automatically handles positive stat point costs (+1 BP per stat pt), negative stat penalty refunds (+1 Extra BP per $-2$ penalty), Damage Reduction (+1 DR per 2 BP, up to +3 standard limit via dedicated steppers or catalog benefits), skill/spell ranks (+2 BP per rank), passive rank cap tracking (soft flag if passive ranks $> 5$), catalog benefits, catalog detriments (with $+5$ extra BP legal cap tracking), and freeform custom perks.
  - Features dedicated **Damage Reduction (DR) Steppers** in Section 2 (*Ability Score & Defense Adjustments*) with `[-]` and `[+]` controls, auto-calculating 2 BP per rank (e.g. +1 DR = 2 BP, +2 DR = 4 BP, +3 DR = 6 BP) with two-way synchronization to the Moderate Benefits catalog.
  - Generates live itemized receipts with exact costs per element.
- **Soft-Limit Non-Enforcement Policy**:
  - Calculates and displays exact points spent, extra points earned, effective budget, and balance in real time.
  - If a build exceeds the budget or standard limits, a high-contrast warning badge (`[OVER BUDGET]`) alerts the player/GM.
  - **Crucial Requirement**: The studio **never blocks** saving or applying over-budget builds. GMs and players retain full freedom for narrative awards, high-powered campaigns, or custom homebrew.
- **Accordion Benefit Drawers**:
  - Collapsible, styled drawers for Minor (1 BP), Moderate (2 BP), Major (3 BP), Extreme (4 BP), and Epic (6 BP) benefits.
  - Integrated search box and tier-filtering pills for rapid scanning across 40+ canonical catalog perks.
- **Archetype & Earth Class Support**:
  - 12 canonical archetype pills (`Arcanist`, `Barbarian`, `Bard`, `Cleric`, `Druid`, `Fighter`, `Mage`, `Monk`, `Paladin`, `Rogue`, `Merchant`, `Necromancer`).
  - Earth Class toggle automatically appends Silver Earth Box with guaranteed Earth Hobby Skill Potion.
- **1-Click Application & Export**:
  - **Create World Item**: Compiles a valid Foundry Item document of type `'class'` into the World Items Directory.
  - **Apply to Crawler**: Embeds the class item directly onto a selected active crawler, updating ability scores and embedding granted skill/spell items automatically.
  - **JSON Export / Import**: Instant backup and sharing across campaigns.
- **Sidebar & Macro Integration**:
  - Injected directly into the Foundry Items Directory sidebar via `[ 🎓 Class Studio ]` button.
  - Global developer shortcut: `window.carl.openClassCreator()`.
  - Canonical system macro included in the `carl-rpg.macros` compendium pack as **"Class Creator Studio"** (`macroKey: "class-creator"`).

## 8. Focus Retention & Scroll Preservation Subsystem

To provide a seamless, non-disorienting UX while editing builds with dozens of perks, ability scores, and options:

### Architectural Principles
1. **Zero Viewport Jump on State Changes**:
   - Every scrollable container (`.dcc-studio-builder`, `.dcc-receipt-list`, `.dcc-studio-sidebar`, `.dcc-studio-layout`, `.window-content`) is indexed via `_saveScrollPositions` before DOM replacement.
   - Restored scroll positions are reapplied both synchronously and via next-frame callbacks (`requestAnimationFrame`).
2. **Precision Focus Retention**:
   - `_saveFocusState` synthesizes unambiguous CSS selectors combining tags, classes, and data attributes (`[data-stat]`, `[data-delta]`, `[data-id]`, `[data-size]`, `[data-heritage]`, `[data-tier]`).
   - `_restoreFocusState` calls `target.focus({ preventScroll: true })`. The `preventScroll: true` flag prevents the browser from forcibly scrolling the window to the focused element, preventing jarring jumps to the top.
3. **Cursor & Text Selection Range Preservation**:
   - Filter inputs, search inputs, and name fields record `selectionStart`, `selectionEnd`, and `selectionDirection`.
   - On re-render, the cursor stays exactly at the typed position, enabling uninterrupted typing and live filtering.
4. **Accessible Button Upgrades**:
   - Accordion drawers use semantic `<button type="button" class="dcc-accordion-header">` elements with `:focus-visible` outline rings for full keyboard accessibility and reliable activeElement capture.

## 9. Character Sheet Race & Class Selection & Reversal System

### Interactive Dropdowns on Page 1 (Core)
The plain text inputs for **Race** and **Class** on Page 1 of the crawler character sheet are upgraded to dynamic `<select>` dropdowns:
- **Race Dropdown**: Categorized into *Earth Races (Silver Earth Box)*, *Alien Syndicate Races (Galactic Popularity)*, and *Custom / World Races*.
- **Class Dropdown**: Categorized into 10 Archetypes (*Arcanist*, *Barbarian*, *Bard*, *Cleric*, *Druid*, *Fighter*, *Mage*, *Monk*, *Paladin*, *Rogue*), *Multiclass & Special Classes*, and *Custom / World Classes*.
- **Custom / Uncataloged Preservations**: If an existing character has a custom race or class string, it is automatically marked with `(Custom)` in the dropdown to preserve campaign lore without data loss.

### Dynamic Application & Automated Reversal Engine (`DCCRaceClassApplier`)
Selecting a race or class immediately applies its benefits to the crawler. When switching or removing a race or class, previous benefits are cleanly undone:

1. **Ability Stat Synchronization**:
   - Stat bonuses granted by the previous race or class are subtracted from both `system.abilities.${stat}.value` and `unenhanced` scores; stat penalties are restored.
   - New stat bonuses/penalties are then applied. Derived DCC stat modifiers and maximum health automatically recompute using official DCC RPG rules (no D&D math).

2. **Non-Item Skill Rank Retention**:
   - When a class or race granting skill ranks is removed or switched, the system computes `remainingRank = currentRank - grantedRank`.
   - **Crucial Rule**: If the crawler has non-item ranks higher than the amount being removed (e.g. natural ranks earned through gameplay, grinding, or story advancement), the skill item is **not deleted**; its rank is simply reduced to the remaining non-item ranks.
   - If no non-item ranks remain (`remainingRank <= 0`), the embedded skill or spell item created by that race/class is cleanly deleted from the character.

3. **Creature Size Scaling**:
   - Selecting a race updates the crawler's size category (`system.attributes.size`) according to the race's official rating (e.g. Pocket Kuma sets Small, Sasquatch sets Large).
   - Removing or switching races reverts the character to their base size or applies the new race's size rating.

4. **Embedded Documents & Flag Tracking**:
   - The engine automatically embeds an official Item document of type `'race'` or `'class'` on the actor and stores clean metadata under actor flags (`appliedRace`, `appliedClass`).
   - Dragging and dropping Race or Class compendium items onto the character sheet automatically invokes `actor.applyRace()` and `actor.applyClass()`.

## 10. Damage Resistance, Movement Speeds & Advantage/Disadvantage Conditions

### Damage Resistance (DR) Integration
- Racial and class Damage Resistance bonuses (e.g. Amazonian `+2 DR`, Tigran `+1 DR Buff`, Igneous `+3 DR`) are parsed and added directly to `system.attributes.dr.buffs`.
- When switching or removing a race or class, the exact granted DR bonus is subtracted from `system.attributes.dr.buffs`, leaving armor, gear, and temporary buffs intact.

### Dynamic Movement Speeds & Modes
- **Walk Movement Deltas**: Races granting move deltas (e.g., Tigran `+10ft Move`) modify the actor's walk speed (`system.attributes.speed.move`) by the parsed delta. Reverting the race subtracts the delta cleanly.
- **Special Movement Modes**: The system supports dedicated movement attributes on actors:
  - `system.attributes.speed.climb`: e.g., Arachnid innate ceiling and wall climb move (20 ft).
  - `system.attributes.speed.swim`: e.g., Crocodilian swim speed (20 ft).
  - `system.attributes.speed.fly`: e.g., Bune, Obsidian Butterfly, Skyfowl flight speeds (20 ft).
  - `system.attributes.speed.burrow`: e.g., Fathom Dwarf stone and earth burrowing (20 ft).
- Upon removing or switching races, granted special movement modes are reset to 0.

### Advantage & Disadvantage as Custom Active Condition Items
In CarlRPG, Advantage and Disadvantage are not passive text notes; they are active, tracked game objects:
- **Advantage on Checks**: Automatically instantiated as custom embedded `Item` documents of type `'buff'`, assigned a permanent duration tagged with `[RACIAL]` or `[CLASS]`, and flagged with `flags['carl-rpg'].grantedBy = 'race' | 'class'` and `flags['carl-rpg'].isAdvantage = true`. Examples include *Advantage: Feline Reflexes* (Cat), *Advantage: High Ground Bravado* (Swashbuckler), and *Advantage: Roadie Rigging* (Professional Roadie).
- **Disadvantage on Checks**: Automatically instantiated as custom embedded `Item` documents of type `'debuff'`, assigned a permanent duration, and flagged with `flags['carl-rpg'].grantedBy = 'race' | 'class'` and `flags['carl-rpg'].isDisadvantage = true`. Examples include *Disadvantage: Clawed Clumsiness* (Tigran), *Disadvantage: Highborn Arrogance* (High Elf vs. dwarves/rat-kin), and *Disadvantage: Cold-Blooded Torpor* (Crocodilian).
- **Automated Removal**: When a race or class is swapped or removed, all condition items granted by that source are deleted from the actor's embedded items list.
